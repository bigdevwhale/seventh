## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- The game: a family cursed to the seventh generation; bean-bag odds from stats, traits, relics and the family legacy; tempers and stones on the soul; births, deaths and marriages year by year; succession to any living descendant; the eldest son's grudge; the dead's last wishes and dreams; the rival family's feud; secrets that surface; relics; the family tree and the chronicle.
- Three lands, each with its own art, names, money, rival, curse and history: Rus (1690–1950, lubok print), medieval Europe (1180–1460, illuminated manuscript), Japan (1662–1950, ukiyo-e print). 75–84 everyday events and 18–22 historical ones per land, chains of consequences that come back years later, and paths a family can take off the plough.
- English and Russian; the language follows the phone and can be switched in Settings.
- A four-page introduction, hints on the first events, and Settings with the StillCraft community links (report a bug, suggest an idea, follow updates, the chat).
